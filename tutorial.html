Viewing Tutorial

01

Install iWebTV

Download iWebTV from your device's app store. It helps reduce intrusive ads and allows easy casting to smart TVs and streaming devices.

02

Open BOHO+

Navigate through the available categories including Movies, TV Shows, Anime, Sports, and Live Channels.

03

Select a Streaming Source

Choose one of the available providers. If one source is slow or unavailable, simply try another option.

04

Start Playback

Some streaming players may require pressing the play button more than once before the media begins loading properly.

05

Cast or Watch

Once the stream loads, you can cast it to your TV, queue it for later, or continue watching directly on your device.

06

Handle Redirects Safely

If a popup or redirect appears, simply close the new tab and return to the original streaming page. Avoid downloads or suspicious prompts.
